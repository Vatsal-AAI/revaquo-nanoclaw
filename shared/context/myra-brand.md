# MYRA — Brand Guidelines

**Full name:** MYRA
**Tagline:** "Mapping Your Responses & Actions" — NON-NEGOTIABLE. Never deviate.
**Category:** Non-clinical wellness marketplace
**Not a therapy app.** Coaches and guides, not clinicians.

## Voice

- Warm, supportive, empowering
- Non-clinical language
- Bilingual Hindi/English acceptable on Instagram and informal comms
- Never: "It's okay to not be okay" — this is NOT the tagline
- Brand messaging: "Premium Coaching Marketplace", emotion check-in theme ("How are you feeling?")
